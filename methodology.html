Student Success Score (SSS) Methodology Note

Multi-Attribute Mathematical Formulation, Weighting Rationale & Risk Framework

KPMG

1. Executive Overview & Institutional Need

Traditional higher education reporting relies almost exclusively on retrospective, single-dimensional indicators such as Semester End Examination (SEE) marks or cumulative grade point average (CGPA). These lagging indicators fail to alert faculty mentors before academic probation or placement disqualification occurs.

The Student Success Score (SSS) is an analytics and AI-powered composite index on a continuous scale of $[0, 100]$. It synthesizes 7 disjoint campus operational systems: Academic Examinations, Biometric Attendance, Learning Management Systems (LMS), Co-Curricular Engagement, Placement Assessment Portals, Skills Assessments, and Faculty Mentorship Feedback.

2. Mathematical Formulation

SSS = 0.35 × AI + 0.25 × ELI + 0.30 × PRI + 0.10 × FWI

A. Academic Index (AI) — Weight: 35%

Reflects foundational subject mastery, weighted by internal evaluations and penalized non-linearly for accumulated backlogs:

AI = clip( 0.55 × (CGPA × 10) + 0.45 × (Internal Exam Score) - [12 × ActiveBacklogs + 2.5 × HistoryBacklogs], 0, 100 )

B. Engagement & LMS Index (ELI) — Weight: 25%

Captures behavioral and daily operational discipline across biometric attendance logs and LMS activity:

ELI = clip( 0.40 × Attendance% + 0.30 × AssignmentCompletion% + 0.15 × LMS_Activity + 0.15 × CoCurricular, 0, 100 )

C. Placement Readiness Index (PRI) — Weight: 30%

Evaluates market employability, competitive problem solving, aptitude, and verbal interview performance:

PRI = clip( 0.35 × CodingScore + 0.30 × AptitudeScore + 0.20 × MockInterview + 0.15 × SkillsRatingScaled, 0, 100 )

D. Feedback & Well-being Index (FWI) — Weight: 10%

Encapsulates human faculty mentor observations and student institutional satisfaction:

FWI = clip( 0.60 × (FacultyMentorRating × 20) + 0.40 × (StudentSatisfaction × 20), 0, 100 )

3. Dual-Axis Risk Categorization Matrix

Students are independently mapped along both an Academic Risk axis and a Placement Readiness Risk axis:

Academic Risk Axis
• High: Active Backlogs ≥ 2, or CGPA < 6.0, or Attendance < 65%, or AI < 50.
• Medium: Active Backlogs == 1, or CGPA < 7.0, or Attendance < 75%.
• Low: No active backlogs, good standing.
Placement Outcome Risk Axis
• High: Coding Score < 42/100, Aptitude < 48/100, or PRI < 50.
• Medium: Coding < 58/100, Aptitude < 60/100, or PRI < 65.
• Low: Strong coding profile, aptitude cleared.

4. Bonus Features: Clustering Personas & Explainable AI (XAI)

Unsupervised K-Means Segmentation

Feature vectors $[AI, ELI, PRI, FWI]$ are clustered using K-Means ($k=5$) and translated into institutional student personas. This surfaces non-intuitive segments such as "High Academic, Low Placement" (high CGPA but failing coding tests) and "Disengaged High-Potential" (high inherent aptitude but missing lectures).

Transparent Explainability Engine (XAI)

Every student record is accompanied by a transparent waterfall of positive driver points and negative risk drag points. Faculty advisors immediately see whether a low score is driven by attendance failure, lack of coding practice, or active backlogs.
