Student Success Score (SSS) Methodology

Mathematical formulation, indicator weightings, and decision intelligence logic.

Composite Scoring Equation

SSS = 0.35 × Academic_Index + 0.25 × Engagement_LMS_Index + 0.30 × Placement_Index + 0.10 × Feedback_Index

The Student Success Score maps each student to an absolute 0–100 continuum. Indicators penalize active backlogs, incorporate continuous LMS logins, and model technical coding aptitude.

Pillar Weight Underlying Indicators Target Objective
Academic Index (AI) 35% CGPA (0-10 normalized), Internal Assessment Marks, Backlog Penalties Identifies degree progression health and semester clearing likelihood.
Engagement & LMS (ELI) 25% Biometric Attendance %, LMS Weekly Logins, Assignment Completion %, Certifications Captures daily student presence and digital coursework engagement.
Placement Readiness (PRI) 30% Competitive Coding Score, Quantitative Aptitude, Mock Interview, Soft Skills Predicts campus recruitment employability and corporate readiness.
Feedback & Well-being (FWI) 10% Mentor Appraisal Rating, Student Satisfaction Index, Course Sentiment Accounts for emotional sentiment, pedagogical satisfaction, and mentorship.
