Scoring Logic

Student Success Score (SSS) Formulation

SSS = 0.35 × Academic + 0.25 × Engagement/LMS + 0.30 × Placement + 0.10 × Feedback
Non-Linear Backlog Penalties: Direct deduction of 12 points per active backlog triggers early alerts before end-semesters.
Dual-Axis Separation: Distinguishes academic retention risks from placement employability risks.
